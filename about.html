About ZimArts

Empowering Zimbabwe's creative voices since 2012

🎨 Our Mission

To nurture and showcase Zimbabwe's emerging artistic talent while preserving and evolving our rich cultural heritage through exhibitions, workshops, and digital media.

👁️ Our Vision

To be Zimbabwe's leading hub for contemporary and traditional arts, connecting local artists with global opportunities.

📖 Our History

Founded in 2012 by a collective of artists, ZimArts has grown from a small studio space to a vibrant cultural center hosting 80+ events annually and supporting 200+ registered artists.
